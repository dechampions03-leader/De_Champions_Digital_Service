# AI platform marks

These compact SVG marks identify the four third-party platforms supported by the DCDS Prompt Studio.

The SVG artwork is sourced from the MIT-licensed [Goku Prompt Hub AI Icons collection](https://github.com/gokuscraper/ai-icons):
- OpenAI / ChatGPT: `svgs/openai.svg`
- Claude: `svgs/claude.svg`
- Google Gemini: `svgs/gemini.svg`
- Grok: `svgs/grok.svg`

Each mark is reproduced as a simple white monochrome vector for legibility on its matching provider-coloured card. Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Their appearance here is solely to identify prompt compatibility and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement by those providers.
