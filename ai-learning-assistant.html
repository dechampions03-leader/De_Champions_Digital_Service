One shared assistant

The student dashboard and authenticated DCDS surfaces open the same protected DCDS AI service. The server verifies the signed-in account, administrator role and approved age/guardian eligibility; the browser cannot select its own role.

Privacy reminderVerified student conversations and optional learning-memory notes are saved privately to the signed-in account and can be deleted from the chat history controls. Your chats are never automatically shared with other students or added to the shared DCDS brain. DCDS checks age eligibility and parent/guardian consent where required before AI processing begins. Do not enter passwords, OTPs, card details, bank information or sensitive personal information. Messages and limited context are processed through the DCDS AI service and safety checks. Read the DCDS Privacy Policy for details about providers and data handling.

Need human support?

For account access, payment disputes, certificates, referrals or payout issues, use the official DCDS support route. I cannot change student records or financial status.
