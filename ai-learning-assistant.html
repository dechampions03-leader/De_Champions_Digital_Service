One shared assistant

The student dashboard and authenticated DCDS surfaces open the same protected DCDS AI service. The server verifies the signed-in account and administrator role; the browser cannot select its own role.

Privacy reminderVerified student conversations and optional learning-memory notes are saved privately to the signed-in account and can be deleted from the chat history controls. Your chats are not automatically shared with other students or promoted into the official DCDS brain. Only verified DCDS students and authorised administrators can use this release. Do not enter passwords, OTPs, card details, bank information or sensitive personal information. Messages and limited conversation context are processed by Google Gemini to generate replies. Gemini free-tier content may be used by Google to improve its products.

Need human support?

For account access, payment disputes, certificates, referrals or payout issues, use the official DCDS support route. I cannot change student records or financial status.
