A CLEAR, SEPARATE PROCESS

How membership and identity work.

Identity approval and paid membership are two different decisions. Both must be valid before the Blue Badge is displayed.

01

Choose your membership term

Select Monthly, Annual or Five-Year. The price and end date are shown clearly before any payment is requested.

02

Select your document country

Choose the country that issued your identity document. Available document types will depend on confirmed provider coverage.

03

Complete identity verification

Use the supported provider's secure verification journey. A failed, incomplete or unsupported check does not grant a badge.

04

Payment is confirmed separately

DCDS independently confirms the membership payment. The badge is eligible only when identity approval and an active entitlement both pass.

Worldwide applicant design: DCDS intends to support applicants internationally. The exact countries, government documents and checks available at launch will be confirmed with the selected provider. Not every document is guaranteed to be accepted in every country. Where automated support is unavailable, the application must follow a safe review route rather than being auto-approved.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Important details before choosing a Verified+ membership term.

Does paying for Verified+ automatically give me the Blue Badge?

No. Payment does not prove identity. The Blue Badge requires a successful, approved identity decision and an active paid membership term. These are checked separately.

Is identity verification included in the membership price?

No. The identity-verification fee is separate from all three membership prices. Its amount has not yet been approved and must be displayed before the identity check begins.

Can people outside Nigeria apply?

DCDS is designing an international applicant journey. Actual country and document acceptance will depend on the selected provider's confirmed coverage. DCDS will show supported choices before an applicant starts.

Is the Five-Year plan a lifetime membership?

No. It is a single upfront payment for 60 months. The entitlement expires after five years unless you choose and pay for a new term. It does not create a perpetual benefit.

Will my membership renew automatically?

No automatic renewal is planned for these terms. You choose whether to renew. DCDS will show the expiry date and renewal terms before checkout opens.

Can Verified+ automatically make me a DCDS seller or Creator?

No. TRUSTMARKET seller approval and Creator recognition are separate decisions. Identity verification and a paid membership do not automatically confer business approval, professional qualifications or Creator recognition.

Can I pay for a membership right now?

Not through this page. This is an informational product preview. Secure checkout and the identity-verification provider are not live yet. Please do not transfer money for these plans until DCDS publishes an official checkout route.
