# DCDS Verified+ Member Network — Brand Content and Enforcement Policy

**Status:** Product policy approved for specification and synthetic-preview work  
**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Scope:** DCDS Verified+ Member Network only  
**Production impact:** None. This document does not create database objects, enable moderation services, alter accounts, or deploy code.

## 1. Purpose and standard

The DCDS Verified+ Member Network is a branded learning, creativity, innovation and professional-development space. It is not a general-purpose social-media wall.

Every original post, comment, reply, reshare, media item and associated caption must contribute directly to DCDS learning, digital skills, creative work, professional growth, DCDS services, or an official DCDS activity.

**Off-brand content is not allowed, even when posted by a paid or identity-verified member. Verified+ status never grants immunity from moderation.** The same content standards apply to Verified+ publishers and standard/unverified accounts that engage with the feed.

## 2. Who can see, publish and engage

| Account state | View approved posts | Original posts | Comments and replies | Reactions | In-network reshare |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Eligible, identity-approved, active Verified+ | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Signed-in standard/unverified DCDS account | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | Yes, attributed card only; no caption |
| Restricted/suspended network account | As restriction permits | No | As restriction permits | As restriction permits | No while restricted |
| Signed-out visitor | No | No | No | No | No |

- Approved posts are visible to **all signed-in DCDS accounts**, whether verified or unverified.
- Only a server-confirmed account with approved identity eligibility and an active Verified+ entitlement may create original posts.
- Standard accounts may react, comment, reply and reshare an existing item inside DCDS. A reshare is an attributed reference card only: no caption, new media, external destination or audience expansion.
- Standard accounts cannot create original posts, upload original post media or add authored commentary through a repost.
- Anonymous/guest feed access is not included.
- This rule does not change public website pages, course access, student records, the existing supervised Student Community or other DCDS permissions.

## 3. Approved DCDS subject matter

Content is allowed only when its main purpose is clearly connected to one or more of these areas:

1. **Digital skills and learning:** DCDS courses, lessons, course questions, skill practice, study guidance, constructive learning discussions and safe learning resources.
2. **Creative and technical work:** graphic design, video editing, AI image/video creativity, animation, web design/development, software, productivity tools and other DCDS-supported digital disciplines.
3. **Projects and portfolios:** a member's own DCDS-related project, work-in-progress, design breakdown, demo, portfolio image, constructive critique request or completed creative deliverable.
4. **Professional development:** skills, career preparation, work habits, professional collaboration and opportunities directly related to the digital skills and services taught or supported by DCDS.
5. **DCDS activities:** official academy, course, event, service, product, platform, programme, support and policy announcements; DCDS challenges and approved campaigns.
6. **Relevant member progress:** a learning milestone or achievement voluntarily shared by the member, provided it does not expose private grades, assessment answers, certificates or another person's information without approval.

A short motivational or celebratory message is acceptable only when clearly attached to a DCDS learning, project or professional-development context. "Positive" by itself does not make unrelated social content eligible.

## 4. Prohibited content and conduct

The following must not be posted, commented, replied, reshared or promoted:

- unrelated personal updates, lifestyle posts, dating/relationship content, party invitations, social meetups, random memes, entertainment chatter or general-purpose social conversation;
- political campaigning, religious solicitation, ideological arguments or unrelated controversy;
- advertisements for unrelated businesses, products, services, courses, groups, jobs, fundraising, investment schemes or other platforms;
- affiliate, referral, promo, coupon, monetisation, recruitment or traffic-driving content not officially authorised by DCDS;
- WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X or other off-platform contact invitations, social handles, QR codes, usernames or requests to move conversations elsewhere;
- unsolicited direct-contact requests, phone numbers, email addresses, personal messaging IDs or contact details;
- links to external websites, social profiles, forms, file shares, URL shorteners, app downloads or invitation pages, except an exact DCDS-controlled domain/path formally allowlisted by DCDS;
- misleading income claims, guaranteed-earning claims, fake testimonials, fabricated credentials, impersonation, scams, phishing or deceptive promotions;
- spam, repetitive posts, engagement bait, artificial reactions, bot activity or coordinated manipulation;
- harassment, bullying, threats, hate, discriminatory abuse, sexual content, exploitation, doxxing, privacy violations, malicious files or illegal content;
- content that bypasses existing age, guardian, privacy, safeguarding or supervised Student Community rules.

A member's own portfolio, GitHub project, design preview or learning resource should be uploaded or presented through an approved DCDS feature where available. An external link is not permitted merely because the linked material is relevant. DCDS may later approve a small, explicit allowlist after review.

## 5. Link, contact and embedded-content controls

- External URLs are blocked by default in post text, comments, replies, profile text used in the network, media captions, QR codes, repost content and attached metadata.
- Only exact DCDS-controlled domains and paths explicitly recorded in a server-side allowlist may pass. Similar-looking names, unapproved subdomains, redirects and shortened URLs do not count as DCDS-controlled.
- Social handles and off-platform contact instructions are prohibited even where no clickable URL is present.
- Media checks should inspect visible text and QR codes where technically supported. Users must not evade the link rule by placing a URL inside an image, screenshot, video frame, watermark, file name or encoded QR.
- A relevant third-party educational resource may be submitted for moderator review. It remains hidden until an authorised moderator approves it and adds the exact destination to the allowlist.
- Never render untrusted URLs as clickable previews before validation. Never fetch arbitrary user-submitted URLs from a server.

## 6. Monitoring and moderation workflow

Apply content checks to every network write surface:

- original posts, edits, media uploads and captions;
- comments, replies and edits;
- reshare metadata and attached previews;
- profile fields and media displayed in the network;
- member reports, repeat attempts, suspicious links and engagement spam.

Required controls:

1. **Before publication:** validate role/entitlement and format; run link, spam, safety and DCDS-topic checks. Clearly prohibited content is rejected or hidden. Ambiguous content is held for human review.
2. **Media inspection:** validate MIME type and size, scan for malicious files, and use OCR/QR inspection where supported. Do not claim these checks are perfect.
3. **After publication:** accept member reports, detect repeated violations and re-check edited or reshared content. A report is a review signal, not proof.
4. **Human moderation:** show authorised moderators the content, applicable rule, relevant context, active strikes and available actions. Require a reason code and audit event for every formal action.
5. **AI assistance:** classifiers may flag or hold content, recommend categories and prioritise queues. AI alone must not impose a permanent ban, revoke paid entitlements, decide identity status or make an irreversible account decision.
6. **Privacy boundary:** monitoring is limited to content deliberately submitted to or displayed in the network, related safety signals and member reports. It must not inspect private AI conversations, private learning memory, private messages, identity documents, payment records or unrelated account data to judge a network post.

## 7. Strike and restriction ladder

For confirmed ordinary off-brand or external-link violations, apply progressive enforcement. Strikes apply to the network surface and do not change the member's course, payment, wallet, payout or VTU records.

| Confirmed event within a rolling 180-day period | Minimum action |
|---|---|
| 1st violation | Remove/reject content, record strike 1 and issue a clear warning naming the rule |
| 2nd violation | Remove/reject content, record strike 2 and block original posting for 7 days |
| 3rd violation | Remove/reject content, record strike 3 and suspend network posting and engagement for 30 days |
| 4th violation | Suspend all Verified+ Network participation for 90 days and send the case to Root Administrator review |

- The 180-day window is rolling. Strikes older than 180 days are not counted toward the ladder, but minimal audit history may be retained under the approved retention schedule.
- Content removal is not automatic membership cancellation. Paid membership, identity status, wallet balances, course access, student records, payouts and VTU transactions are separate systems and must not be changed by network strike logic.
- A restriction must state its scope and end date. A posting restriction must not silently become a platform-wide account ban.
- DCDS may apply a stronger action for a single severe incident.

## 8. Immediate severe-incident response

For credible threats, doxxing, child-safety concerns, sexual exploitation, targeted harassment, serious impersonation, fraud, phishing, malicious links or other serious safety incidents:

1. hide content and disable relevant network actions immediately where necessary to protect members;
2. place the account in a temporary network safety hold if justified;
3. preserve only the minimum evidence needed for secure, access-controlled review;
4. notify the authorised Root/Safety Administrator promptly;
5. require human review before a long-term or permanent restriction, except where a temporary emergency hold is needed to prevent immediate harm.

A permanent DCDS account ban requires a documented Root Administrator decision and must not be triggered solely by an automated classifier. Network restrictions remain separate from unrelated platform entitlements unless a separate authorised security decision is documented.

## 9. Appeals and transparency

- Every formal strike or restriction must notify the affected account with the content reference, rule category, action, duration and appeal route, without exposing reporters or private evidence.
- A member may appeal within 14 calendar days of notification.
- An authorised reviewer who did not make the original decision should review the appeal where staffing permits.
- If overturned, remove the strike, restore affected network privileges and retain a correction event in restricted audit history.
- Repeated abuse of the report system may itself be moderated, but a good-faith report must not be punished merely because it was not upheld.
- DCDS should publish clear examples of allowed and disallowed content before launch.

## 10. Admin roles and audit

- Moderators may hide/remove content, issue warnings and apply approved short restrictions within delegated permission.
- Only the Root Administrator or specifically delegated senior safety authority may approve a permanent network ban or permanent DCDS account-level action.
- Moderators must not review private identity evidence unless their separate verification role authorises it.
- Record moderator, target account, content reference, rule code, action, duration, timestamp, appeal outcome and override in a restricted, append-only audit trail.
- No moderator may approve their own appeal or bypass age-cohort safety boundaries.

## 11. Launch acceptance gates

Before real accounts can publish or engage, demonstrate:

- server-side role enforcement: standard accounts can view and engage but cannot create original posts or captioned reposts;
- feed visibility for authenticated standard and Verified+ accounts, with signed-out visitors denied;
- external-link denial across posts, comments, replies, profiles, media and QR/OCR-capable surfaces, with exact allowlist tests;
- topic, spam, harassment and safety pre-publication checks with human review for uncertain cases;
- strike escalation, restriction expiry, appeal reversal and append-only audit coverage;
- moderator least privilege and Root-only permanent-ban approval;
- no effect on Student Community age/guardian controls or private AI, student, identity, payment, wallet, payout or VTU data;
- fail-safe behaviour when moderation or allowlist services are unavailable: do not silently publish unchecked content.
