Request account deletion

This page lets you submit an account-deletion request for De Champions Digital Service without needing to install the app.

Before you submit

We will verify that you control the signed-in account. DCDS will review personal data connected to your account and handle any information that must be retained for payment, payout, security or legal audit purposes. Account deletion is not completed automatically when this request is submitted.

Important: deletion removes access to your student account. Any transaction or payout records that DCDS is required or permitted to retain may be kept in a restricted form. Do not submit this request if you only want to sign out or change your email address.

Sign in to the DCDS account you want to delete, then return to this page.
