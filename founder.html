A FOUNDER'S STORY

From early ideas to a wider vision

This biography is based on Jeremiah's own account. It shares the public story of his background, interests, values and the mission he is building through DCDS.

01 · ROOTS AND FAITH

A foundation that travelled with me

I was born in Delta State and grew up in Edo State, Nigeria. My parents brought me up with Christian values and taught me about Jesus Christ while I was under their care. I am deeply grateful for their guidance, and I pray that God rewards the sacrifices and good example they gave me.

As I grew older, I came to understand how deeply a parent's example can influence a child's choices. My faith became an important part of how I approached difficult circumstances and the decisions before me.

I believe in loving God, serving Him, trusting Him and treating neighbours with kindness. I want my work and leadership to remain grounded in those values.

02 · THE CREATIVE MIND

A childhood drawn to making things

From an early age, I loved creativity. I enjoyed experimenting with ways to power lights, making toy cars, designing, editing and quickly absorbing new information.

I once dreamed of becoming an engineer because I had ideas about automobiles and electrical systems. I was fascinated by how things worked and how an idea could become something practical.

That curiosity did not disappear. Over time, it found new expression through digital design, video editing and technology.

03 · LEARNING THROUGH CHALLENGE

Keeping the desire to learn

My educational journey was affected by financial hardship and changes in my school environment. There were difficult moments, but I remained focused on science because I still hoped to become an engineer.

One achievement I remember is taking first position in my secondary school. I was hesitant to share the news because I worried about how some fellow students might respond.

Education taught me to value knowledge. Even when I could not afford to continue formal education as far as I wished, I kept learning independently and seeking ways to develop my abilities.

04 · DISCOVERING DIGITAL CREATIVITY

Learning tools that opened new possibilities

My interest in digital technology grew as I began experimenting with creative applications and looking for ways to learn more.

Canva and PixelLab helped me explore graphic design, while CapCut and Filmora introduced me to video editing workflows. These tools gave me practical ways to express ideas and develop useful skills.

05 · THE IDEA BECOMES A MISSION

Why I started De Champions Digital Services

Before DCDS took shape, I tried advertising to attract clients to a group. The approach was not working as I had hoped, and the money spent on advertisements made me reconsider how I could create something more sustainable and useful.

During private periods of reflection and prayer, I began thinking beyond my own immediate needs. I wanted to build something beneficial to people: a place where those who felt lost in their vision, dreams or goals could find encouragement, learn practical skills and develop their abilities.

I also describe the name De Champions as arising from a personal spiritual prompting. I embraced the name and connected it to a larger sense of responsibility and purpose. That developing vision became De Champions Digital Services.

Building digital ideas has brought technical challenges, sleepless nights, financial limitations and lessons from decisions that did not work out as expected. I have continued learning, developing ideas and coaching others along the way.

“I want people who have lost direction in their dreams, visions and goals to rediscover their potential and begin moving forward.”
06 · LEADERSHIP AND VALUES

Leadership through dignity and patience

I do not see leadership as permission to look down on people. I want everyone around me to feel respected, whether they are students, colleagues, moderators, ambassadors or people just beginning their journey.

I believe in treating people as I would want to be treated. I want to be remembered as a calm, patient and kind leader who listens, shows endurance and maintains fairness and accountability.

07 · A GLOBAL AMBITION

Empowerment through knowledge

My dream is for De Champions Digital Services to become a source of skills empowerment around the world. I want DCDS to keep learning, adapting and introducing useful discoveries as opportunities and technology evolve.

I want young people to value formal education while also acquiring practical digital knowledge. Learning should not stop when a person leaves a classroom.

08 · A MESSAGE TO THE NEXT GENERATION

Keep learning. Keep trusting. Take the next step.

To anyone facing a difficult beginning, I want to say: do not give up simply because your circumstances are challenging. I have experienced seasons when I had dreams but lacked the resources to pursue them. I know how discouraging that can feel.

Trust God, value education and keep developing practical skills. Do not surrender yourself to destructive influences. Be patient with your growth and take the steps that are within your reach.

I believe life should not be treated as a race in which everyone must jump from one to ten. Progress takes preparation, learning, discipline and time. Your journey does not have to look exactly like someone else's.

“Your present circumstances do not have to define the limits of your future. Keep learning, keep growing and keep moving with purpose.”
09 · THE LEGACY I HOPE TO BUILD

A name associated with impact

I want the name Jeremiah Chuks Godspower, known as De Champions, to represent impact, encouragement, vision, kindness, playfulness and a focused mind.

I want the values behind my work to remain meaningful beyond my own lifetime. I hope future generations will understand the importance of acquiring skills, developing themselves, sharing knowledge and treating people with dignity.

My first spiritual ambition remains to serve God throughout my life. I see worldly positions and possessions as temporary, and I want the work I do to be useful to people without becoming more important than my faith.

The journey is still unfolding.

De Champions Digital Services is being built around a continuing commitment to creativity, learning, innovation and practical skills empowerment.
