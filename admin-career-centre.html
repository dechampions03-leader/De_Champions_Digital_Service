Career Operations Overview

This area summarises engagement with DCDS career support. It does not claim that a student has been hired unless the student reports the outcome.

Recommended operating practice: verify mentor consent and professional background before activation; verify every opportunity before publishing; respond to mentorship requests promptly; and ask students before using their outcome information in any public success story.
