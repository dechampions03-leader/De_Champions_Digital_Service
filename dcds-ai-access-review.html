DCDS AI access review

Review age-band requests and verify adult guardian consent where required. Access is enforced by the Edge Function; changing a screen or browser value cannot unlock the assistant.

Important safeguards: Store age bands only, not full dates of birth or identity-document images. Approve ages 13–17 only after a separately verified adult guardian account and documented consent are confirmed. Guardian approval alone does not activate AI chat: the separate DCDS teen-release gate remains off until youth-safety disclosures, monitoring and escalation controls are approved. Under-13 accounts remain blocked until DCDS has an approved Zero Data Retention arrangement and the additional child-safety requirements are complete. Never place identity numbers or private family details in review notes.
