Before entering the DCDS Community

Please read and accept the community rules. DCDS spaces are for learning, respectful collaboration and safe professional networking. Your age cohort is kept separate, group chat is moderated, and live sessions are hosted by authorised DCDS administrators.

Community rules
  • Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, hate, threats or impersonation.
  • No scams, unofficial money requests, investment promises or selling schemes.
  • Never ask for or share passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, bank details or government ID numbers.
  • Do not share external links in group chat, private contact details or another person's confidential work.
  • Respect age-separated rooms. No attempts to contact or move conversations across age cohorts.
  • Use report and block controls when needed. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.
  • Project posts and profile visibility may be reviewed by DCDS before classmates can see them.
  • Live sessions are scheduled by DCDS. Do not record, screenshot or redistribute a session without explicit permission.
  • DCDS may hide content or suspend community access while a safety concern is reviewed. Students may contact DCDS to appeal a decision.

Community Group Chat

Group rooms only — no private student-to-student messages. External links, money requests, password/OTP requests and payment solicitation are not allowed. Teen messages are reviewed before classmates can see them.

Never upload: passport, government ID, national identity number, bank statement, payment card, password, OTP or confidential family information. DCDS credential review is not a substitute for official identity verification.

Scheduled DCDS Live Events

Events are scheduled and hosted by authorised DCDS administrators. Teen events have an additional administrator co-host. Recording is disabled. Your meeting link is shown only after you register for an event in your own age cohort.
