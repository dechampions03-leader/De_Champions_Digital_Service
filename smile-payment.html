Smile Network Payment

Verify the Smile account email, select the correct linked account, choose a live data bundle and pay securely through Flutterwave.

1. Verify your Smile account

Enter the email address registered with Smile. We will ask VTpass to verify it and return the available account IDs. Your Smile account password is never requested.

2. Select a bundle

Plans and prices are retrieved from the VTpass Smile catalogue. The checkout re-verifies the email and selected account before payment.

Loading Smile bundles…

3. Payment details

Your selected Smile account ID receives the bundle. The contact phone is supplied as the customer contact number. Foreign-currency estimates use daily reference rates with no DCDS FX markup; the server confirms the final amount before Flutterwave checkout. No purchase is submitted until payment is confirmed.

Choose a Smile bundle to see the estimated checkout amount.
